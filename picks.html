NFL Picks

Against the spread

Enter the home team's spread as you see it. If your number is lower than the line, you take the home team at the line; if higher, the away team. Matching the line exactly is no pick. Lines stay hidden until you submit, and submitted picks are final.

Group picks

Everyone's numbers, sorted by edge: how far the group's average is from the line. When everyone who entered a number is on the same side, that's a group pick. A game's picks show once you've picked it or it kicks off.
